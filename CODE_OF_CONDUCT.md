# Code of Conduct

Our engine reads how people treat each other in conversation. We hold our own threads to the same standard.

## Expected

- Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Give reasons. A judgment without grounds is not a review.
- Ask before concluding, and assume good faith until shown otherwise.
- Protect other people's data. Personal information, and text from our sources, stays out of issues, commits and chat.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, or demeaning remarks about anyone's identity, background or ability.
- Threats, intimidation, or sustained disruption of a discussion.
- Publishing someone's private information without consent.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in every Lumanic AI repository and project space, and whenever someone represents the project elsewhere.

To raise a concern, contact the maintainers of the repository privately. If you cannot find a private channel, open an issue asking for one and leave out the details. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may restrict or ban participants who break this code.
